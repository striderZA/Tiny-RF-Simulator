# Power Meter

The Power Meter reports the total power of the signal at one output pin. Open it from **View > Power Meter**.

## Choose a source

The **Source** list starts with **(none)**. After it, the list has one entry for each output pin in the circuit. Each entry is the component's label followed by **OUT**. A second output of the same component is labelled **OUT2**.

Choose the output whose power you want to read.

## Readings

- **Total Power: <level> dBm** is the total power at the source.
- **Total Power: -inf dBm** means the power is zero.
- **Unavailable: <reason>** means no reading can be made. For example, the reason is **no source selected** while the Source list shows (none).

## Saving

The Source choice is not saved with the project. Choose the source again after you open a project.
