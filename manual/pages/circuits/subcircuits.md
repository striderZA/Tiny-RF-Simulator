# Subcircuits

A subcircuit groups several components into one labelled block. Use it to keep a large chain readable: collapse the parts of the circuit you do not need to see, and expand them when you do.

## Create a subcircuit

Drag a rectangle across empty canvas to select the components you want to group. This is a rubber-band select. When you release the mouse button, a dialog asks for a name.

Type a name, then choose **Create**. To leave the components ungrouped, choose **Cancel**.

## Expand and collapse

A collapsed subcircuit is drawn as one block. Click **Expand** on the block to show the components inside.

To expand or collapse a subcircuit from its menu, right-click it and choose **Expand** or **Collapse**.

## Rename a subcircuit

Right-click the subcircuit, choose **Rename**, type the new name, then choose **Rename** to confirm. Choose **Cancel** to keep the old name.

## Remove a subcircuit

Right-click the subcircuit and choose **Ungroup**. The block is removed, and its components stay on the canvas.
