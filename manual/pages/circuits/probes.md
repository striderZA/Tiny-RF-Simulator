# Probes

A probe sends the signal at a pin to the **Spectrum Analyzer**, so you can see its spectrum while you change the circuit. Probes are saved with the project.

## Add a probe

Hold Ctrl and click an output pin. The Spectrum Analyzer (**View > Spectrum Analyzer**) plots the signal at that pin. The tooltip on a pin reads "Ctrl+click: probe (show in Spectrum Analyzer)".

Ctrl+click a component's body to probe its first output.

If a component has more than one output, Ctrl+click the pin of the output you want to probe. Each output pin can be probed separately.

## Remove a probe

Shift+click a probed pin to remove its probe.

## Limits

You can have up to four probes at a time. Each probe's trace uses its own colour.
